About this map

The EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) only lets cocoa, oil palm and rubber onto the EU market if they were not grown on land deforested after 31 December 2020. This map shows where Ghana's tree crops in 2025 sit on land that was forest on that date.

How it is made

  • Tree crops in 2025 come from a 10 m land-cover map of Ghana made with TabPFN-3.5 (Prior Labs) from Google Satellite Embeddings. Cocoa, cashew, rubber, oil palm and mango are grouped as tree crops.
  • Forest in 2020 is the EU's own reference map, JRC Global Forest Cover 2020 (version 4). It counts land with at least 10% tree cover as forest, so it includes woodland savannah in the north.
  • Confidence is how sure the model is about each pixel. Where it is 0.6 or more, the map was right 90% of the time in testing; below 0.6, about half the time. That is why overlaps are shown in two colours, and why the land-cover map can fade the areas where the model is unsure.

Keep in mind

This map helps decide where to look; it does not prove compliance or deforestation. The 2020 forest map can count shaded cocoa as forest, and the 2025 map can mix up tree crops and forest. Check flagged areas on the ground.
